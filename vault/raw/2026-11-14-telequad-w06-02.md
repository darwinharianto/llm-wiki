Such very short transmissions mainly target usage cases requiring low latency, such as some URLLC (Ultra Reliable, Low Latency) services. In specific cases, transmissions can be carried out over only a fraction of a slot, with the minimum set to only two symbols. Transmissions are usually carried out over one slot. This can be configured semi-statically or it can change dynamically as part of the scheduling decision. In the case of TDD, and as to allow for flexible traffic adaptation, each OFDM symbol in a slot can be classified as 'downlink', 'uplink' or 'flexible' (i.e. either downlink or uplink). NR supports both FDD and TDD operation with the same frame structure. When the SCS is doubled, i.e. 30 kHz, the OFDM and CP lengths are approximately devided by two compared to the 15kHz SCS. for 15 kHz SCS, the OFDM symbol duration is approximately 66.6 µs and the CP length is approximately 4.7 µs. The extended CP is approximately four times longer than the normal CP and is used for cells having large delay spread. Note that, for the 60 kHz SCS, an extended CP is possible.
