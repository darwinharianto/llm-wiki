# Telecom Reference Sheet — 3GPP 23009-g00: Enhanced SRNS Relocation

Ingested: 2026-11-14T05:19:49Z

6.2.3.1.2	Enhanced SRNS Relocation The successful operation of the Enhanced SRNS Relocation procedure is as follows.

After transmission of the IUR-ENHANCED-RELOCATION-RESPONSE message RNS-B and RNS-A act as follows: After 3G_MSCA has received the IU-ENHANCED-RELOCATION-COMPLETE-REQUEST message from RNS-B, it shall start to configure the necessary Iu resources for the RNS-B and send the IU-ENHANCED-RELOCATION-COMPLETE-RESPONSE message to RNS-B.
