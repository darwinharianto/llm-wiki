# As We May Think (Memex Excerpt)

Author: Vannevar Bush
Year: 1945
Publication: The Atlantic Monthly

## Summary

Vannevar Bush envisioned the **Memex**, a mechanized private file and library device in which an individual stores all books, records, and communications, and which is mechanized so that it may be consulted with exceeding speed and flexibility.

## Associative Indexing and Trails

The essential feature of the Memex is **associative indexing**, the basic idea of which is a provision whereby any item may be caused at will to select immediately and automatically another. This is the essential feature of the Memex: the process of tying two items together is the important thing.

The user builds a **trail** through the material — inserting marginal notes, comments, and links between documents. Wholly new forms of encyclopedias will appear, ready-made with a mesh of associative trails running through them.

## Relevance to Modern AI Knowledge Bases

Bush solved the conceptual model of associative trails, but left open who performs the continuous clerical maintenance of linking, indexing, and revising trails as collections scale. Modern LLMs act as the tireless archivist that maintains the trails.
