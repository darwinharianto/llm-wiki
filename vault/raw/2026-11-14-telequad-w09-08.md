The pages I will post will also be about those wireless communication technology between the mobile clients and an access point. As the terminology implies, WLAN is also a kind of LAN (Local Area Network), so it use most part of the standard LAN protocol especially in higher layer. The only difference is very low layer parts (PHY and Low MAC). Most of the contents I will deal with will be about PHY/MAC part of the protocol stack.
