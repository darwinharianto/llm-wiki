# 3GPP 23009-g00: Enhanced SRNS Relocation (Protocol Summary #6)

Ingested: 2026-11-14T05:19:41Z

In figures 11a and 11b, the resources are released by using the IURELEASECOMMAND sequence. After 3G_MSC-A has received the IU-ENHANCED-RELOCATION-COMPLETE-CONFIRM message from RNS-B, it shall begin to release the resources associated to the RNS-A. After the completion of the Iu UP initialization, RNS-B shall send an IU-ENHANCED-RELOCATION-COMPLETE-CONFIRM message to 3G_MSC-A. After RNC-B has received the IU-ENHANCED-RELOCATION-COMPLETE-RESPONSE message, it shall start to configure the Iu transport bearer for each Radio Access Bearer between the MSC-A and RNC-B and perform Iu UP initialization, if necessary. If the necessary resources cannot be allocated or a failure occurs in 3G_MSC-A, it shall send an IU-ENHANCED-RELOCATION-COMPLETE-FAILURE message to RNS-B. After 3G_MSCA has received the IU-ENHANCED-RELOCATION-COMPLETE-REQUEST message from RNS-B, it shall start to configure the necessary Iu resources for the RNS-B and send the IU-ENHANCED-RELOCATION-COMPLETE-RESPONSE message to RNS-B.
