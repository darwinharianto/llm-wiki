# Telecom Reference Sheet — End of an era as RAN6 closes

Ingested: 2026-11-14T05:19:54Z

At the recent RAN (#88e) plenary meeting, Working Group 6 - responsible for the GERAN and UTRAN radio and protocol work - was formally closed. The closure of RAN6 marks the end of a significant era for the mobile industry.

As 4G evolves to 5G, we are safe in the knowledge that the GERAN and UTRAN standards, and the core network behind them are completely stable and can continue to be used for as long as there is a need. Note: update made July 9, to change title.
