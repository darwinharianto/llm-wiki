The operator may activate an equipment trace for any subscriber's equipment (IMEI) from an OSF by invoking the management function Activate Equipment Trace in one or more VLR in the HPLMN. 5.3.2	Tracing of IMEI via VLR This TS describes one method of handling IMEI tracing i.e. tracing of IMEI via the VLR. An IMEI may be traced in order to find out the current IMSI, or the location or behaviour of faulty or stolen equipment reported via the EIR. If the tracing of IMEIs is implemented then the way the trace facility is used and organized, including restrictions due to national laws and regulations, will be a matter for the PLMN Operator. 5.3.1	General 5.3	Equipment Tracing (Tracing of IMEI)
