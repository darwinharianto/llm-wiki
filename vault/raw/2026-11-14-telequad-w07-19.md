7.12	Exceptional Procedures 7.12.1	Roaming into a network not supporting CAMEL Phase 3

MT calls to any profile will be received by the subscriber (subject to call forwardings and call barrings provided in the gsmSCF on the called profile), although no indication of the called profile will be received. The HLR will not allow OR, this means that for MT calls, the GMSC will always support CAMEL Phase 2, allowing the gsmSCF to invoke appropriate Call Forwardings and Call Barrings.
