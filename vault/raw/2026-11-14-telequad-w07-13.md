5.3	Equipment Tracing (Tracing of IMEI) 5.3.1	General

If IMEI trace is required, it is the responsibility of the network operator to specify that CIPHER-MODE-COMPLETE contains IMEIs, or optionally the IMEI is called for in connection with MOC, location update etc. Alternatively the network can ask the MS for the IMEI by sending a TS 24.008 [19] IDENTITY REQUEST message to the MS, indicating that the IMEI is required.
