## Key Takeaways

1. **Associative Indexing**: Information retrieval should mirror human thought by tying items together along associative trails rather than rigid hierarchical folders.
2. **Personal Knowledge Trails**: Scholars build reusable trails with marginalia that turn raw books into living encyclopedias.
3. **Bridge to AI Wikis**: The conceptual blueprint for [[vannevar-bush-memex|Vannevar Bush Memex]] directly anticipates [[compounding-knowledge-architecture|Compounding Knowledge Architecture]] and modern LLM-maintained knowledge bases.
