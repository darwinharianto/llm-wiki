# Vannevar Bush Memex

Described in [[source-as-we-may-think|As We May Think (1945)]], the **Memex** was Vannevar Bush's vision for an intimate, private, mechanized library where an individual could store books, notes, and records and bind them together via **associative indexing**.

## Associative Trails vs. Web Hyperlinks

Unlike the public World Wide Web that emerged decades later, the Memex emphasized:
- **Private curation** over public broadcasting.
- **Persistent associative trails** connecting disparate documents with personal commentary.
- **Connections as first-class artifacts** equally valuable as the source documents themselves.

## The Missing Archivist Problem

Bush accurately anticipated associative linking, but the Memex still assumed the human owner would manually index and maintain every trail. In [[llm-wiki-pattern|LLM Wiki Pattern]], the LLM assumes the clerical archivist role so the human can focus on curation and inquiry.
